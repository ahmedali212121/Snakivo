How to Play Snakivo

Snakivo is a realtime multiplayer snake arena for desktop and mobile. Your goal is simple: eat food, grow larger, survive, and climb the leaderboard.

Movement and Boost

Use your mouse on desktop or the mobile joystick to steer. Hold Space or use the Boost button for extra speed. Boosting costs a small amount of mass, so use it for escapes and chases.

Food and Rare Events

Food comes in different sizes and values. Common food gives small growth, while larger and golden food gives much more. A rare Golden Food event appears periodically and is marked on the mini map.

Eliminations

Larger snakes can eliminate smaller snakes on contact. Kill streaks award bonus points, and the current leader is marked with a crown.

Skins and Coins

Earn coins by playing and claim a daily reward. Skins can be unlocked with coins or, where available, an optional rewarded advertisement. Country skins include a special Saudi Arabia design.

Bots

Bots are off by default. You can enable them from the home screen if you want an active practice arena when few human players are online.
