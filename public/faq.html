Snakivo FAQ

Is Snakivo free?

Yes. Snakivo is designed to be playable for free in a modern web browser.

Does Snakivo work on mobile?

Yes. The game includes a mobile joystick and a dedicated Boost button.

Are bots required?

No. Bots are off by default and can be enabled before entering a match.

Can I play in Arabic?

Yes. English is the default language and Arabic can be selected from the interface.

What are rewarded ads?

Rewarded ads are optional advertisements that may grant an in-game reward after the advertising provider confirms that the ad was completed. No live rewarded ads are enabled until an approved provider is connected.

Do purchases give combat power?

Snakivo is designed to keep cosmetic skins separate from competitive skill. Coins primarily unlock cosmetic content or optional convenience features.
