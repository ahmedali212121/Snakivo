Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Snakivo may process information when you use the game.

Game session data

Temporary gameplay information such as your chosen nickname, score, size, connection identifier and match state may be processed to provide realtime multiplayer gameplay.

Local storage

Your browser may store preferences and progression locally, including language, sound preference, selected or unlocked skins, coins, rewarded-ad count and daily-reward status. This information is stored on your device unless a future account system is introduced.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another approved advertising service is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their policies and applicable consent requirements. Snakivo will enable required consent tooling before personalized advertising is used where legally required.

Data minimization

Snakivo is designed to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Do not use sensitive personal information as your public nickname.

Your choices

You can clear browser storage using your browser settings. You may also choose not to use optional rewarded advertising.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page.
