Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Snakivo handles information when you use our online multiplayer game and website.

Game Session Data

Temporary gameplay information such as your chosen nickname, score, snake size, connection identifier and match state may be processed to provide real-time multiplayer gameplay.

Browser Storage

Your browser may store game preferences and progression locally, including language, sound settings, selected or unlocked skins, coins, rewarded-ad activity and daily-reward status. This information remains on your device unless a future account system is introduced.

Advertising, Cookies and Google AdSense

Snakivo may use Google AdSense and other approved advertising services. Advertising providers may use cookies, device information or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertising. Where required by law, users will be provided with appropriate consent choices before relevant advertising technologies are used.

Rewarded Advertising

Some optional game features may allow players to voluntarily view an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward. Rewarded advertising is optional and players can continue using the standard game experience without choosing a rewarded advertisement.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services used for hosting, analytics or advertising may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Data Minimization

Snakivo is designed to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Players should not include sensitive personal information in public nicknames.

Your Choices

You can clear locally stored game data through your browser settings. You may also decline optional rewarded advertising and use available consent controls where applicable.

Children's Privacy

Snakivo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Players should avoid sharing personal or sensitive information through nicknames or gameplay.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Snakivo's features, advertising services or legal requirements change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@snakivo.online.
