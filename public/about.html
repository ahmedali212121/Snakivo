About Snakivo

Snakivo is an independent browser-based multiplayer snake arena built around quick competition, growth, movement skill and cosmetic progression.

Players collect food of different values, compete for rare Golden Food events, use boost strategically, hunt smaller snakes and climb the live leaderboard. Snakivo supports desktop and mobile controls, English and Arabic, optional bots, country skins, coins and daily rewards.

Our approach

The game is designed to remain easy to start while giving experienced players room to improve through movement, timing and risk management. Cosmetic unlocks are intended to add variety without replacing skill.
