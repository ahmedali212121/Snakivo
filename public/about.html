About Snakivo

Snakivo is an independent browser-based multiplayer snake arena built around fast competition, growth, movement skill and cosmetic progression.

Players collect food with different growth values, compete for rare Golden Food events, use boost strategically, hunt smaller snakes and climb the live leaderboard.

Gameplay

Snakivo combines classic snake-style gameplay with online multiplayer competition. Larger food rewards create races between players, while boost, positioning and movement give skilled players more ways to compete.

Features

The game supports desktop and mobile controls, English and Arabic, optional bots, country-inspired skins, virtual coins, daily rewards, leaderboards and cosmetic progression.

Our Approach

Snakivo is designed to be easy to start while giving experienced players room to improve through movement, timing, positioning and risk management. Cosmetic unlocks are intended to add variety without replacing player skill.

Development

Snakivo is an independently developed game and will continue to evolve with gameplay improvements, new cosmetic content and additional features.
